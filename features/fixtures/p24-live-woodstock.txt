##### Victorian Charm Meets Strong Returns in Woodstock
Positioned in the heart of Woodstock, this character-rich Victorian residence presents a rare blend of heritage, design, and income potential.

Soft, well-proportioned interiors are complemented by intimate sleeping areas and full bathrooms, creating a collection of self-contained spaces that feel both private and cohesive.
